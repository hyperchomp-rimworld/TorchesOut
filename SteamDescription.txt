[h1]TorchesOut[/h1]
Sick of torches burning with no colonists around and using up all your wood? Well, now they don't.

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*]The last colonist out of a room puts out its torches, and the next one in lights them back up.
[*]Prisoners, and patients in medical beds, are never left in the dark.
[*]Outdoor torches go out during the day.
[*]At night, outdoor torches light up as soon as someone comes into their light radius, so they don't waste fuel when no one's around.
[*]Mark any torch "Always on" to keep it lit no matter what.
[*]Torches you switch off yourself stay off.
[/list]

[h1]Mod Options[/h1]
[list]
[*]Relight torches when a colonist walks into a dark room.
[*]Only put torches out once a room is completely empty.
[*]Only keep outdoor torches lit near colonists.
[*]Turn off outdoor torches during the day.
[*]Update interval: how long a room stays empty before its torches go out, and how often outdoor torches check for daylight and nearby people.
[/list]

[h1]Save Compatibility[/h1]
Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.

[h1]Performance[/h1]
Built with performance in mind: torches only react when someone changes rooms, and outdoor torches check for daylight and nearby people every 30 ticks (configurable in the mod options).
